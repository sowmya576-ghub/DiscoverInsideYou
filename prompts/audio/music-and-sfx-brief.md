# Music and SFX Brief Template

Copy into a video's `audio-editing.md` when briefing a composer, searching a licensed
library, or generating audio with a tool. Record every final choice in `assets-manifest.md`.

```markdown
### Music brief
- Mood arc: <e.g. curious → building wonder → calm resolution>
- Tempo / energy: <e.g. ~80–90 BPM, sparse>
- Instrumentation: <e.g. soft synth pads, plucked textures, light pulse; no vocals>
- Must leave room for: narration (mid frequencies clear)
- Avoid: trailer-style hits, aggressive drops, fear/horror tones
- Length: <seconds>, loopable? <yes/no>
- License requirement: original / licensed library / platform-permitted — record terms

### SFX list
| Cue | Scene | Biological action | Description | Level |
|-----|-------|-------------------|-------------|-------|
| FX1 | SC## | <e.g. muscle contracts> | <soft, tight organic tension> | subtle |
```
