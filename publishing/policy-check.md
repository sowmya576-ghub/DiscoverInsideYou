# Platform Policy and Originality Check

YouTube policies change. **Check the current official policy pages before publishing.**
Don't rely on this file as the policy itself. Record the date you checked in each
package's `youtube-publishing.md`.

## Check per video

| Area | What to check | Where to look |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Altered or synthetic content | Whether the video's AI-generated or realistic synthetic visuals/voice need disclosure | YouTube Help: disclosing altered or synthetic content |
| Medical misinformation | Claims don't contradict health authority guidance; no treatment or diagnosis claims | YouTube medical misinformation policy |
| Copyright | Music, SFX, footage, images all owned, licensed, or platform-permitted | YouTube copyright and fair use pages; license terms in `assets-manifest.md` |
| Made for kids (COPPA) | Deliberate audience setting. The channel serves all ages, but that doesn't automatically make content "made for kids" | YouTube Help: determine if your content is made for kids |
| Monetization (if applicable) | Original, non-repetitive content with meaningful educational value | YouTube Partner Program policies |
| Thumbnails and titles | Not misleading; no shocking or graphic medical imagery | Community Guidelines |

## Originality rules

- Original narration, storyboard, and scene design for every video.
- Never copy another creator's script, storyboard, signature sequences, or thumbnails.
- Research can draw on the same science; the expression must be ours.
- Templates are for consistency, **not** for mass-producing near-identical videos with only
  the topic changed. Each video needs its own story shape, visuals, and insight.
- No promises of monetization or reach anywhere in the repo or videos.

## AI-generated assets

Record in `assets-manifest.md` which assets are AI-generated (visuals, voice, music) and
which tool made them. This record supports disclosure decisions and license tracking.
