# Titles, Thumbnails, Descriptions

## Titles
- Accurate and descriptive; the video delivers exactly what the title promises.
- Curiosity from the real question ("Why does your skin get goosebumps?"), not from shock or
  vague teasing ("You WON'T believe what your skin does").
- No unsupported superlatives, fake urgency, or medical claims.
- Write five options, pick one, and note the reason (`publishing.title_options`).

## Thumbnails
- One focal point, strong contrast, 0–4 words of text.
- Cinematic realistic 3D only, from or matching a real scene in the video.
- No misleading anatomy, gore, or exaggerated disease imagery.
- Prompt template: [`prompts/visuals/thumbnail-prompt-template.md`](../prompts/visuals/thumbnail-prompt-template.md).

## Descriptions
- First two lines: the hook and the answer in brief (what shows above "more").
- Sources: the sources behind the main claims, with direct links.
- Standard disclaimer from `publishing.disclaimer` in `config/channel.yaml`.
- Chapters for long-form.

## Hashtags and tags
- A few relevant hashtags (Shorts: include `#Shorts`).
- Tags: relevant phrases only, no stuffing. They don't guarantee reach.

## Pinned comment and CTA
- A pinned comment that adds value: a follow-up question, a sourced bonus detail, or a
  correction note.
- At most one natural call to action, woven into the ending. No pressure language.
- Sign-off "Discover Inside You — your body, your world, uncovered." used selectively.
