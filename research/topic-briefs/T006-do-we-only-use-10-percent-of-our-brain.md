## Audience question

Is it true we only use 10% of our brains?

## Current scientific answer (preliminary)

No. Brain imaging shows activity throughout the brain, and over a day virtually all regions
are used. Damage to even small areas has lasting effects, and an organ that uses roughly
20% of the body's energy while making up about 2–3% of body weight is unlikely to sit 90%
idle. The myth likely spread through misattributions (a 1936 self-help preface citing
William James; an apocryphal Einstein quote) and misread early neuroscience.

## Hook ideas (must be accurate)

- Answer first (don't withhold): "No — and your brain's energy bill proves it."
- Not every region is active at once, and that's normal. Avoid the opposite overclaim ("100% all the time").
