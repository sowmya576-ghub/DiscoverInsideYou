# Topic Prioritization

Score each candidate 1–5 on each criterion and record the total in the `priority_score`
column of [`backlog.csv`](backlog.csv). Scores are editorial judgments, not predictions.
**Never invent search volumes or predict virality as fact.**

| Criterion | 1 | 5 |
|-----------|---|---|
| Curiosity question | Few people would wonder | Many people have asked themselves this |
| Broad-audience value | Niche | Relevant to almost everyone |
| Evidence quality | Weak or contested | Well established, with good sources |
| Visual demonstrability | Hard to show | The mechanism is vivid in 3D |
| Originality / differentiation | Heavily covered with nothing new to add | Fresh angle or deeper mechanism |
| Opening potential | No natural hook | Accurate, striking first line exists |
| Complete explanation possible | Can't resolve in format length | Satisfying answer fits the format |
| Connection potential | Isolated | Links naturally to other episodes or a series |

Total: 8–40. Use `priority` = high (≥ 32) / medium (24–31) / low (< 24) as a starting
point, then apply judgment: category balance, timeliness, production effort.

**Weak evidence ≠ rejected.** A topic with mixed evidence can still make a great video if
the uncertainty *is* the story ("Why do we yawn? Scientists still disagree"). It must say so.

Avoid near-duplicates: before adding a topic, search the backlog for overlap.
