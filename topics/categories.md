# Content Categories

Category codes are defined in `content_categories` in
[`config/channel.yaml`](../config/channel.yaml) and used in `topics/backlog.csv` and
each `package.yaml`.

| Code | Category | Scope | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|-------|
| A | Everyday body mysteries | Reflexes and reactions people notice daily | Strong Shorts fit |
| B | Brain and nervous system | Neurons, memory, learning, attention | Often needs Simplified-model labels |
| C | Heart, blood, and breathing | Circulation, lungs, oxygen | Avoid fear-based heart imagery |
| D | Digestion and metabolism | Gut, enzymes, energy | Keep it tasteful for all ages |
| E | Sleep and dreams | Sleep stages, circadian rhythm, dreaming | Much is still debated. Flag hypotheses |
| F | Senses and perception | Vision, hearing, touch, taste, smell, illusions | Great visual potential |
| G | Immunity and healing | Immune cells, inflammation, wound repair | No treatment advice |
| H | Skin, bones, and muscles | Structure, movement, growth, repair | |
| I | Human biology misconceptions | Myths and corrections | Don't repeat a myth without correcting it |
| J | Deeper scientific explainers | Long-form mechanisms across systems | Usually long-form |

Adjacent human-biology topics (development, aging, genetics) fit when they stay
science-focused. Assign the nearest category and note it.

Balance: aim to rotate categories so no single one dominates a month. Check the backlog's
`category` column before choosing the next topic.
