# Series

A series groups related videos (e.g. a playlist on the senses). Each video still lives in
`content/shorts/` or `content/long-form/`; the series just links them. Set `series:` in each
`package.yaml` to the series ID.

Track series in [`series.csv`](series.csv). Each video must stand on its own. A series
connects videos but never requires watching another to understand one.
