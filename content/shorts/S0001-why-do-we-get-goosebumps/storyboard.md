# Storyboard — S0001 · Why do we get goosebumps

Format: 9:16 vertical · 1080×1920. Each scene serves one narration line or idea.
Full prompts: `visual-prompts.md`. Times are estimates from the word count (≈150 wpm).
Re-time them from the recorded narration.

## Scale journey

Forearm skin (body/macro) → through the surface into a translucent skin cutaway (tissue) →
nerve signal reaches the muscle (cellular, simplified) → contraction seen in cutaway and on
the surface → furry mammal coat (comparison) → back to sparse human arm hair → into the
follicle bulge for stem cells (micro, mouse study) → pull all the way back to a person
listening to music. The viewer always enters and exits a scale through the same follicle,
so orientation is never lost.

## Continuity notes

- Recurring subjects: the human forearm (SC01, SC06, SC08) and the slanted follicle + arrector pili cutaway (SC02–SC04, SC07). Use the continuity bible descriptions verbatim.
- Lighting direction: soft key from upper left, cool cyan rim from right throughout.
- Screen direction / camera axis: hair slants toward screen right in every follicle shot.
- Palette usage: navy depth in all cutaways; cyan only for the nerve signal and stem-cell activation; red-orange only for a faint warmth haze (SC05–SC06) and muscle tissue tone.
