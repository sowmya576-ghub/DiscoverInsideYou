# Brand Guide — Discover Inside You

Settings (colors, tagline, handle) live in [`config/channel.yaml`](../config/channel.yaml).
This guide explains how to use them.

## Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Channel | Discover Inside You |
| Handle | @DiscoverInsideYou — **pending confirmation** (`channel.handle_status`) |
| Tagline | Your body. Your world. Uncovered. |
| Sign-off (selective) | "Discover Inside You — your body, your world, uncovered." |

**Mission:** make the science of the human body fascinating and understandable through
scientifically accurate explanations, cinematic 3D visuals, curiosity-driven storytelling,
and memorable discoveries.

**Audience:** curious viewers of all ages. Clear language a general audience understands,
without making the channel childish.

## Color

| Role | Value | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Background | Midnight navy `#071827` | Environments, title cards, thumbnail backgrounds |
| Primary accent | Luminous cyan `#00D9F5` | Nerve signals, highlights, focus glows, key label |
| Secondary accent | Anatomical red-orange `#FF572E` | Blood, muscle, heat, emphasis (sparingly) |
| Text | White / light gray | Labels, on-screen text, thumbnail text |

- Cyan and red-orange are accents, not fills. Most of the frame stays realistic tissue
  color against navy depth.
- Never recolor anatomy to the point it becomes inaccurate (e.g. blood stays red).

## Logo and banner

The official logo and banner are the identity references. They live in
[`brand/logo/`](logo/README.md). Do not recreate, redraw, or alter them. If a variant is
needed (e.g. watermark), request it from the owner and record it in that folder's README.
